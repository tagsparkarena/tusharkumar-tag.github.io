A free local multiplayer tag game for two, three or four friends. Share a keyboard, grab a power-up and pass the spark before time runs out.

Play TagSpark Arena

Desktop or laptop keyboard required. Everyone plays on the same device—this is not online multiplayer. No game account or download needed.

How to play

Open the lobby and press your jump key to join. One player starts as “It.” Touch another player to pass that status to them. When the timer reaches zero, the player who is It loses the round; everyone else earns a round win. Most round wins wins the match, and tied players share the victory.

A default match lasts three 90-second rounds. A short tag lock prevents immediate tag-backs. Hold jump for a higher leap, tap for a short hop, or use your action key to drop through a one-way platform. Press Escape to pause.

Keys can be remapped in the lobby. Use the keyboard tester before starting: some keyboards cannot detect every combination of simultaneous presses.

Three ways to turn the chase around

  • Speed Burst: a temporary 20% speed boost lasting four seconds.
  • Invincibility Shield: three seconds of tag protection for runners.
  • Double Jump: an extra mid-air jump while the eight-second power-up is active.

Power-ups are temporary. Look for a different route when someone gains an advantage, and watch for their effect to expire.

Pick your playground

Play across Sunlit Courtyard, Rooftop Garden, Clockwork Crossing, Crystal Cavern and Cloud Playground. Their platforms, moving routes, bounce pads and portals give you different ways to chase or escape. Custom matches let you choose maps, round length and modifiers, including Low Gravity, Slippery Floors, Power-Up Rain, Moving Platform Mayhem, Super Bounce and Speedy Round.

Privacy and advertising

The game saves preferences, controls and gameplay statistics locally in your browser. It does not require a game account or send those gameplay statistics to a game server. This homepage does not load advertising or analytics scripts. It includes an AdSense ownership-verification tag; this does not itself display ads.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process connection information under its own privacy statement. If advertising is introduced later, the privacy information and consent controls will be updated before ads are enabled.
